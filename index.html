How Governance Ecosystem Scoring Works
Lyra scores uploaded documents against the Governance Ecosystem Baseline — a composite of the treaty articles and verification frameworks that define the minimum governance requirements for a functioning safeguards ecosystem.
PRESENCE
Is the governance concept addressed anywhere in the document?
SPECIFICITY
Are requirements concrete enough to guide implementation?
ENFORCEABILITY
Are verification, audit, and consequence mechanisms defined?
GAP SCORE
Composite deficit between document coverage and baseline threshold. Feeds directly into Level III indicator sliders.
