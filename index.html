Auracelle Lyra · Human vs Agentic AI Governance Matrix Wargame
Decide under uncertainty. Negotiate under constraint. Stress-test whether safeguards governance survives.
Play a four- or six-round safeguards governance crisis as the human decision-maker while an autonomous Agentic AI Red Team observes your choices, selects counter-strategies, adapts across rounds, and learns from prior players in the same role. Every move requires an argument, receives an AI adversary move, and is adjudicated before the underlying governance and assurance engines update.
Decision layer first. Analyst layer second.

During play, Lyra hides raw optimization effects so the player cannot simply game the score. After each move, the Agentic AI Red Team selects a state-aware counter-strategy informed by both current-session memory and role-conditioned experience from prior sessions. The adjudicator exposes the analytical rationale, and an expert can override it with a recorded justification.

MODEL INTERPRETATION · Lyra is a research and decision-support environment. Model scores, thresholds, and adjudications are experimental analytical outputs—not official IAEA safeguards conclusions, legal determinations, or substitutes for expert judgment.
